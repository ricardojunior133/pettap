# PetTap Vision

## Summary

PetTap exists to make the path home simpler for lost pets. It brings together a carefully designed physical NFC tag and a clear digital rescue experience, so a helpful stranger can act with confidence in a stressful moment.

## Mission

Help every pet find a safer way home through simple, dependable technology and an experience people trust when it matters.

## Vision

To become the most considered recovery companion for modern pet families: a product that feels beautiful in everyday life and indispensable in an unexpected moment.

## The Problem

Traditional pet tags are limited by the physical space on the tag. Contact details can become outdated, engraving can wear, and a single phone number may not be enough in an emergency. Meanwhile, a person who finds a pet needs an immediate, understandable way to help—not another app, setup process, or unclear decision.

## Why PetTap Exists

Pets are family. Their identification should be treated with the same care as the collar they wear and the information people rely on to bring them home.

PetTap replaces the pressure of fitting everything onto a small piece of metal with a simple physical gesture: tap the tag with a phone. The resulting rescue page is designed to make the next action obvious.

## Who We Serve

### Pet owners

Owners who want an elegant, personal product that supports their pet beyond basic identification.

### The person who finds a pet

Someone who may be outside, short on time, and uncertain what to do. PetTap must communicate clearly in seconds and make contacting the owner easy.

### The PetTap team

Designers, engineers, operators, and future collaborators who need a shared product standard rather than a collection of disconnected screens.

## Values

### Care before complexity

If a decision makes a rescue moment harder to understand, it is not the right decision. Reduce friction before adding capability.

### Calm confidence

Urgency should be clear without becoming alarming. The product should guide people with composure and empathy.

### Useful by design

Every element must earn its place. We favour fewer, clearer choices and meaningful defaults over crowded interfaces.

### Respect for the pet and owner

Information should feel personal and protected. We communicate only what is useful to help a pet, especially on public rescue pages.

### Quality in the details

The physical tag, the configurator, and the digital profile should all feel like parts of the same premium product.

## Experience Principles

### Make the product feel real

The Studio should present a physical, premium 3D-printed product rather than a flat graphic. Configuration choices should appear to affect the tag immediately.

### Make the rescue action obvious

The public rescue profile prioritises a pet’s identity and contact actions. Lost Mode must be unmistakable while remaining calm and respectful.

### Make ownership feel organised, not administrative

The Dashboard and Pet Workspace should feel like a thoughtful companion app. They should not resemble a dense back-office control panel.

### Design for one-handed, real-world use

Mobile readability, generous touch targets, contrast, focus states, and short action paths are essential—especially for rescue flows.

## Premium Positioning

PetTap is a premium personalised pet accessory and recovery experience. Its value is not only NFC technology; it is the confidence created by considered physical design, calm digital guidance, and an experience that treats pets as family.

Premium does not mean decorative excess. It means restraint, material honesty, reliable interaction, generous space, and a consistent experience at every stage.

## Differentiators

- A personalised NFC pet tag paired with a purpose-built public rescue profile.
- A premium, product-led configuration experience for a physical 3D-printed tag.
- A public experience designed around fast contact, not account creation.
- A distinct Lost Mode presentation for moments that need immediate attention.
- Owner-facing foundations that can grow with a pet over time.

## Notes

- PetTap is not a generic tag catalogue or an administrative dashboard product.
- The public rescue experience uses mock data in the current implementation.
- The project does not currently provide production authentication, data storage, profile editing, or notifications.

## Future Roadmap

Future work may introduce authenticated owner accounts, real profile management, tag activation, multiple pets, health and vaccination records, emergency-contact management, Lost Mode controls, community reporting, notifications, and a history of meaningful recovery events. These capabilities remain future work until implemented and documented.
